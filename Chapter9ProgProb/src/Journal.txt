How does the PriorityQueue know which company is the smallest?

The PriorityQueue knows which company is smallest because the Company class implements Comparable. The compareTo method tells Java to compare companies based on their size. Since PriorityQueue is a min-heap by default, the company with the smallest size automatically gets placed at the front of the queue.

What would we change if we wanted to merge the largest companies first?

We would reverse the comparison in the compareTo method. Instead of comparing this.size to other.size, we would compare other.size to this.size. This would make the PriorityQueue prioritize the largest companies first.
